## Fixed workload and correctness boundary

The suite retained eight balanced process pairs, seed `1949524847`, three
excluded warmups and twelve measured cycles per worker. The fixture retained
256 normal threads, one archived write thread, 3,000 messages, 1,500 history
items and 1,024-byte reasoning payloads. Fixture creation, migration and index
preparation occurred outside timed workers. Cold means a fresh process,
service acquisition and its first five operations; module loading and result
fingerprinting are outside that sum. The OS page cache was not flushed or
claimed cold. Warm APIs include their normal IPC, serialization and worker
messaging costs.

| accent_matches     | 20             |

SQLite retained its stock WAL/NORMAL reader/writer-worker configuration.
Native omdb retained Immediate durability. Every timed visit changed its
timestamp, read the complete row inside the transaction and waited for commit
acknowledgement. The stronger native commit setting remains visible in the
measured cost; it was not weakened to make the comparison pass. Rollback and
committed reopen are required reliability checks, not a power-failure test.

The benchmark fixture's equal outputs are a scoped result. The later expanded
18-case sidebar differential demonstrated that cross-thread steering-message
lookup and remote-child presence semantics were not fully covered by the
earlier checks. The corrections and duplicate-JSON-member cases are documented
in the linked sidebar report. The fixed performance fixture and thresholds
remain unchanged; passing this fixture cannot stand in for those added cases.

## Functional scope and next decision

The existing hosted normal hook, native/adapters and full controlled Linux
application/recovery gates passed. That result is limited to the tests and
flows actually exercised; the later sidebar failures demonstrate why it must
not be described as complete application parity. It establishes no other-platform
or real-provider qualification. The performance and final qualification jobs
failed, and existing main remains unchanged.

Correctness work takes priority: retain the failing sidebar cases, apply the
narrow reviewed corrections and rerun the affected production boundary plus
the required hosted gates. The separately published history/search experiments
remain unqualified until their own results are complete. No workload, sample
count, confidence rule or strict threshold is loosened to obtain a pass.
